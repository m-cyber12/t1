# Privacy policy — Coding Harness

Coding Harness runs entirely on your device.

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- Your projects, shell history and files stay in your device's storage. Files the
  app writes for diagnostics (`setup-result.txt`, `bootstrap-error.log`,
  `exec-audit.txt`, `sealed-payload.txt`) live inside the app's private
  directory and are readable only by you and the app.
- The installable build uses the network only to fetch signed packages from the
  public Termux repository when you run setup or install a pack. The Play build
  fetches nothing at runtime: its toolchain ships inside the app and optional
  packs are delivered by Google Play.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
